# 3D Computer Vision

## From images to geometry

How can photographs reveal the shape of a scene and the motion of a camera? This book develops the geometric ideas behind that question through the first six CS 532 lectures.

Read in lecture order, beginning with the [notation guide](foundations/notation.md) and [prerequisite refresher](foundations/prerequisites.md). Worked examples will connect the lecture slides to the course quizzes. The [quiz index](reference/quiz-index.md) shows the planned placements.

```{note}
This edition establishes the book's structure and authoring conventions. Lecture chapters are planning outlines; their explanations and examples are not yet written. Completed material will be added one lecture at a time.
```

## How to study a section

1. Start with the visual question and explain the intuition in your own words.
2. Read the formula's assumptions and symbol definitions before following the derivation.
3. Work through the application procedure, then attempt the example without its solution.
4. Use the chapter checklist and formula reference to identify what still needs practice.

The website and printable edition are built from the same Markdown pages. Essential explanations and solutions belong in both editions.
